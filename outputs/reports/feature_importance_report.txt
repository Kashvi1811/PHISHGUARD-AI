--- CYBERSECURITY DOMAIN RATIONALE FOR TOP INDICATORS ---

[+] SSLFINAL_STATE:
    Assesses whether the HTTPS certificate is issued by a trustworthy Certificate Authority (e.g. VeriSign, GoDaddy) and has valid duration. Phishing sites typically lack trusted certificates or use self-signed certs.

[+] URL_OF_ANCHOR:
    Measures the proportion of <a> anchor tags in the webpage pointing to an external domain or null javascript/empty hashes (#). Phishing pages often copy legitimate layouts but strip anchor links.

[+] PREFIX_SUFFIX:
    Detects presence of dash symbols (-) in the domain name. Phishers frequently insert dashes (e.g., paypal-security.com) to impersonate trusted corporate brands.

[+] WEB_TRAFFIC:
    Measures domain web traffic ranking via Alexa / SimilarWeb. Legitimate popular websites rank high; disposable short-lived phishing sites have near-zero ranking.

[+] HAVING_SUB_DOMAIN:
    Counts domain dot hierarchy. Legitimate domains have 1 or 2 dots, whereas phishers use deep multi-level subdomains (e.g. verify.bank.com.phish.ru) to deceive users.

[+] LINKS_IN_TAGS:
    Examines the ratio of internal vs external URLs inside <meta>, <script>, and <link> tags. Phishing templates often hotlink legitimate site scripts to appear authentic.

[+] REQUEST_URL:
    Calculates the percentage of external objects (images, videos, multimedia) loaded from an external domain. Phishers load media directly from the real brand domain.

[+] DOMAIN_REGISTRATION_LENGTH:
    Checks if domain registration expires within 1 year. Over 90% of phishing domains are purchased on 1-year disposable contracts.

[+] SFH:
    Server Form Handler inspection. If an authentication form submits credentials to a blank URL ("about:blank") or external server, it is a high-risk credential harvester.

[+] HAVING_IP_ADDRESS:
    Checks if an IP address (or hexadecimal notation) is used directly instead of a domain name in the address bar (e.g. http://192.168.1.1/login.php).
